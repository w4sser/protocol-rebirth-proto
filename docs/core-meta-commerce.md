# Protocol Rebirth — Core, Meta & Commerce Contract

> **Single source of truth for the connected target design.** Owner-approved direction, 7 November 2026 (M19D). Documentation only; not evidence of shipped behavior. This contract supersedes conflicting core/meta/commerce proposals. [Decision register](design-decisions.md) retains the detailed approvals and history; [game reference](game-design-reference.md) describes the inspected prototype.

## Approved experience

**Choose a bunker Project at home → make meaningful route and loot decisions in a raid → choose when to extract → bank the actual haul → visibly restore home → pursue the next ambition.**

| Rule | Approved contract |
| --- | --- |
| **Project and material hunt** | At home, select the overarching bunker Project. Resource targeting happens through meaningful **in-raid route choices and loot probabilities**, with understandable likely sources and risk/reward tradeoffs. No guaranteed requested item, auto-grant at extraction N, or home menu that reserves a material drop. Project tracking is guidance, not a loot entitlement. |
| **Major room restoration (M26)** | Major bunker rooms have **2–3 clearly visible, visual-only restoration stages within the existing TOTAL resource cost**. Allocate each original material requirement across stages so their sum equals the existing total. **No gameplay function, stats, room rewards or other mechanical utility unlock at intermediate stages**; all existing room functionality unlocks at **full completion**. No extra mandatory grind, currency or full-cost repetition. **M37 requires all materials for an individual step to be available at home before one deliberate action consumes them and restores that step; no partial funding or automatic build.** Exact stages, cost split and visual treatment remain open. |
| **Material commerce eligibility** | Resource purchases become available **only after Fabricator L1 has been earned and built through gameplay**. Core L1, first ordinary extraction, a success count, a partial Fabricator stage or ability to afford construction alone does not qualify. Before completion, show **no material purchase offers**, including repair shortcuts, supply kits, mixed bundles or a paid resource-bearing Growth Fund. Fabricator L1 cannot be bought into existence. |
| **Earlier cosmetics** | Cosmetic monetization may appear only after the established D3.9 milestone: first successful ordinary extraction **and its result summary**, with progression shown first. Before gameplay-built Fabricator L1, eligible offers are **cosmetic-only**. Free naming/customization remains optional; nothing commercial interrupts FTUE. |

The no-guaranteed-item rule concerns ordinary material hunting. Preserve D3's authored two guaranteed introductory Scrap Alloy for Core L1; do not remove that explicit FTUE exception.

## Risk, progression and ownership remain intact

- **D1:** Valuable deployed gear and exposed cargo are at risk until extraction. One Secure Slot unlocks at Vault L1; **D1-A locks exactly one protected item unit (not a stack) occupying ordinary bag capacity, with no extra space**. **D1-B allows ordinary material/valuable units only and excludes weapons, armor (even found/unequipped), tools, consumables, and dedicated scouting/mission keys.** Exact taxonomy remains open; **D1-C approves player-selected, replaceable and clearable protection**, while exact mobile/inventory UX remains unresolved. **D1-C.2 now approves automatic, exactly-once return of the protected unit to the home stash after failed raids, shown on the result with no claim or extraction-only objective credit**; **D1-D now approves safe resume of the same raid when recoverable, otherwise one authoritative definitive outcome reconciled once**; **D1-D.1 now locks a limited and clearly communicated return window**; exact duration, raid timer, storage and UX remain open. No BIT item rescue, insurance, paid bag recovery or extra paid protection. Safe home stash and permanent progression/cosmetics persist. Always allow a viable free Basic Carbine retry.
- **D2:** At least one physical return extraction is available from the start, independently of personal-goal completion. Deliberate short cancellable extraction; five seconds is initial test tuning. Required nodes open forward depth progression; continuing never banks cargo. Deeper opportunities and a distinct final payoff remain. Scouting/world-access requirements cannot be bought away.
- **D3:** Fight home, enter the bunker to finish the authored intro, discover BIT, spend intro loot on Core L1, then pursue Fabricator. FTUE ends after the first successful **ordinary** extraction, not Fabricator construction. Genuine first-raid failure and approved contextual assistance remain.
- Bunker progression opens the world; Operator progression supports playstyle; gear supplies risky tactical choices; BIT supports the player. Keep early UI simple while preserving passive felt speed growth and bag burden. No new wallets or compulsory advancement tracks.

## Approved Secure Slot quantity and capacity (D1-A)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** After **fully completing Vault L1**, the only Secure Slot can protect **exactly one eligible item unit**, **not an entire stack**. The unit still occupies its **normal bag space/capacity**; protection grants **no extra cargo space** or additional capacity and does not protect the remainder of a stack. A player cannot multiply protection by consolidating items into one stack.

**Preserved D1 contract:** All other exposed loot and risked equipment follow the ordinary death/extraction outcome. No BIT loot rescue, insurance, paid bag recovery, additional paid slot, automatic extraction or guarantee of survival is introduced. D1-A governs **one unit and ordinary bag occupancy**; D1-B locks the eligible categories; **D1-C later locks player selection, switching and clearing**, not the exact selection interface. **D1-C.2 later settles the core failed-raid reconciliation rule: one protected unit automatically back to home stash exactly once, no claim.**

**Explicitly still open:** Item-category edge cases within the **approved D1-B** restrictions; **D1-C** exact mobile/inventory interaction and result presentation/remaining reconciliation edge cases; **D1-C.2** now locks automatic one-time failed-raid return. **D1-C.1 now approves select/switch/clear whenever the bag is accessible, even under active danger, without a pause or safety benefit**; **D1-D** resume-first policy is approved, while recovery windows/timers, state authority and interrupted-session UX remain open. Exact inventory UI/implementation are not approved, and current code is not claimed to implement D1-A.

## Approved Secure Slot item eligibility (D1-B)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** The **single protected unit** may be an **ordinary construction/material item or an ordinary valuable** carried in the raid. The Secure Slot cannot protect **weapons or armor**, whether equipped, found or carried unequipped (including deliberately unequipped gear). **Tools, consumables and dedicated scouting/mission-key items are excluded** as well. The player must not evade the rule by moving excluded items between equipment and bag slots.

**Preserve D1-A/D1:** One eligible **unit**, never a stack, occupies normal bag capacity; the Slot becomes functional only after full Vault L1 completion. All other possessions remain exposed under D1 until real extraction. No extra slot, insurance, BIT rescue, paid recovery, guaranteed extraction or credit for completing a separate objective is created.

**Still unapproved:** Exact category-specific item IDs and classification edge cases (ordinary materials versus dedicated quest/scouting keys), **D1-C** exact touch/inventory interaction and detailed result/reconciliation edge cases (**D1-C.2 approves automatic once-only protected-unit return after failed raids**; **D1-C.1 approves timing whenever the bag is accessible, even near enemies, without a pause or immunity**), **D1-D** exact interruption recovery windows/technical implementation and all concrete UX. The resume-when-safe policy is approved. No gameplay implementation.

## Approved Secure Slot player selection and switching (D1-C)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** During a raid, the **player explicitly chooses** the one eligible item unit to protect with the Secure Slot. The player may **switch protection** to a different eligible unit or **clear** the Slot. Only **one unit is protected at a time**: on switching, the former selected unit **immediately loses protection** and is again at risk. The game must not automatically value items and silently choose or swap protection on the player's behalf.

**Keep D1-A/B and extraction risk intact:** Fully completed Vault L1 grants one Secure Slot, never a second slot, stack-wide protection or extra bag capacity. Only ordinary materials and valuables are eligible, not equipment, tools, consumables or dedicated keys. Clear player-facing feedback must distinguish protected from exposed items without promising that selection fulfills a mission, grants extraction success, restores a lost bag or duplicates an item.

**D1-C.1 later approves when the player may act:** whenever the raid bag can be accessed, including under threat, without pausing the raid, invulnerability or guaranteed safety. **Still open:** exact mobile/inventory interaction and other bag-access restrictions, handling when a protected item is used/removed, and **result UI and remaining settlement/reconciliation edge cases**; **D1-C.2 approves automatic, exactly-once return of the protected unit after raid failure without a claim or extract-only objective credit**. D1-D's safe resume-first contract remains separate and is now approved; exact time/technical details stay open. No particular inventory gesture, HUD redesign or runtime implementation is approved.

## Approved Secure Slot combat-availability timing (D1-C.1)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** The player may **select, switch or clear** the protected unit whenever the inventory/bag is **accessible during a raid**, including with enemies in the vicinity or an active threat. **Combat does not introduce a safe-only lockout.** The action **does not pause the world, stop enemy attacks, confer invulnerability or guarantee safety**. Managing loot under danger is a deliberate risk, not an automatic defensive effect.

D1-A protects one unit within ordinary bag capacity; D1-B permits ordinary materials/valuables only; D1-C requires player-driven selection, with the former unit losing protection when changed. **Still open:** exact touch controls/UX and bag availability in other states, handling removal/consumption of a selected item, exact result UI/reconciliation edges, and D1-D technical recovery window/timeout/UI rules (the resume-when-safe policy is approved). **D1-C.2 locks automatic return of the protected unit to home stash exactly once after failed raids, with clear result feedback and no claim.** No code/GUI implementation authorized.

## Approved Secure Slot settlement after a failed raid (D1-C.2)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** On raid failure (death, timeout or deliberate abandonment), the **single player-selected eligible protected unit automatically enters the safe bunker stash exactly once**. The **result screen clearly identifies the rescued item**; there is **no separate claim action, pending-reward pool, or second transfer**. With nothing protected, this grant is empty. A real successful extraction already banks the actual carried haul and cannot duplicate the selected unit.

**Separate possession from success:** Keeping the one protected unit **does not turn the failure into a successful extraction**, satisfy extract-required missions or scouting conditions, preserve the rest of the bag, generate an additional loot reward, or grant paid recovery/insurance. D1-A one-unit/no-stack and normal capacity, D1-B eligibility, and D1-C/C.1 manual choice under danger stay locked.

**Still open:** Exact results layout/feedback, classification/consumption/removal edge cases, details of idempotent result storage and currency-valuables conversion, and **D1-D's still-open technical recovery/timeout treatment** for crashes, backgrounding and disconnections before an outcome has been established; D1-D now locks trying to resume the same raid when safely possible, otherwise settling once. No specific inventory or backend implementation is approved.

## Approved interrupted-raid recovery policy (D1-D)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** On an **involuntary interruption** (app crash, disconnection or backgrounding), **attempt to resume the same ordinary raid if its preserved state can safely be restored**, carrying forward actual equipment/loot risk, the player-selected Secure Slot unit and already-earned legitimate objective/raid progress. Unintentional interruption is **not automatically deliberate abandonment**. Do not let a forced relaunch roll the game back to an advantageous checkpoint.

**If safe resumption is impossible:** Establish **one definitive, authoritative outcome** from legitimately preserved raid state, then **settle it exactly once** under D1. Confirmed failure uses D1 loss and D1-C.2's **one protected eligible item unit saved to the home stash once**, with result feedback and no claim or extract-only objective credit; a legitimately completed extraction follows its normal one-time successful settlement. Do not grant automatic extraction, invulnerability, full-bag rescue, arbitrary compensation or extra protection. Force-closing must never enable save-scumming, outcome changes or duplicate rewards. Deliberate Abandon remains a failed raid.

**D1-D.1 later locks a bounded, clearly communicated recovery period, after whose expiry one definitive outcome is reconciled once. Still open:** Exact duration, recoverable-state thresholds, background/raid-timer behavior, expiry priority, persistence/authority, Resume/final-result UX, mobile constraints and anti-replay tests. No guarantee that reconnect is always possible and no gameplay implementation is implied.

## Approved bounded interrupted-raid recovery period (D1-D.1)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** A safely recoverable interrupted ordinary raid stays **available to resume for a limited, clearly communicated period**, not indefinitely. If the player does not return before expiry, determine **one definitive authoritative outcome from legitimate raid state** and reconcile it **exactly once** under D1/D1-C.2. A genuinely completed extraction keeps its real success outcome; a confirmed failure loses exposed loot and returns only the one eligible protected item unit. The expiry itself grants **no free extraction, insurance, saved bag or extraction-required mission credit**.

D1-D continues to require resumption of the **same** safely restored raid while eligible, retaining real loot/equipment risk, objective progress and Secure Slot selection. The recovery window is **not a promise of pause immunity, safe combat, a rewind or guaranteed connection**, and relaunching must not reset or bypass it.

**Still open:** Exact window length/start, deadline communication, the now-approved D1-D.2 real-time original raid deadline, with D1-D.5 now settling at the first legitimate terminal event, while same-tick ordering remains open; authoritative saved-state storage, platform constraints and UX/anti-abuse details. Documentation only.

## Approved original raid deadline continues through interruption (D1-D.2)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** The ordinary raid's original **extraction deadline continues counting in real elapsed time** during app crash, lost connection or backgrounding. D1-D.1's bounded recovery window is about **return availability**, not additional raid time; no interruption freezes or extends the original deadline or undoes a legitimate timeout.

Safely resume the **same** recoverable raid when D1-D permits, preserving loot, equipment risk and selected Secure Slot unit. A legitimately timed-out raid cannot be resumed to extract late; determine and settle **one authoritative result once** under D1/D1-C.2. No free extraction, immunity, whole-bag rescue or duplication.

**D1-D.8 later approves real, verifiable combat exposure through interruption**: ongoing threats may cause damage/death, but no safe frozen rollback and no fabricated unverified damage. **Still open:** the enemy/world simulation method, clock/persistence authority, fairness/recoverability, timeout/expiry precedence, recovery duration and UX. Documentation only, no game code.

## Approved one unresolved ordinary raid and explicit abandonment (D1-D.3)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** A player can have **only one unresolved ordinary raid**. Before a new ordinary raid may begin, any interrupted raid must be **resumed and resolved when safely recoverable**, **deliberately abandoned**, or already have **one authoritative settled outcome**. Reopening the app, viewing the home menu or entering the bunker **must never silently abandon the earlier raid**. There is no parallel unresolved raid and no automatic forfeiture triggered by opening Play.

**Integrity with D1 and earlier D1-D:** Explicit Abandon is a normal **failed raid** with exposure/loss under D1 and **automatic, exactly-once return of only the selected eligible protected unit** under D1-C.2. Established real extraction or timeout is settled once under its proper outcome. D1-D resumes the same safely recoverable raid, D1-D.1 limits its return availability, and D1-D.2 keeps its **original extraction deadline running in real time**, even while the player is in home screens. No extra survival, loot, extraction or free recovery is granted.

**D1-D.4 now resolves return-screen priority:** foreground Resume or Explicitly abandon for safely recoverable pending raids, or the real result if already final, without automatic action or clock pause. **Still open:** exact touch UI/confirmation, timing copy, duration, timeout priority, multi-device persistence and errors. No game code or runtime behavior is changed by this decision.

## Approved focused return choice for interrupted raids (D1-D.4)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** When reopening the game with a **safely recoverable unresolved ordinary raid**, the player first sees **Resume the existing raid** or **Explicitly abandon it**, **ahead of the normal home/bunker and Play flow**. Explain that **the original raid deadline keeps counting in real elapsed time** and the separate **recovery-availability period is limited**; it never adds extraction time. If an **authoritative final outcome already exists**, show **the real result instead**, not the outdated choice.

**No silent action or protection:** No option is auto-selected. Opening the app, returning home or showing this choice **does not implicitly abandon the raid**. This choice **does not pause the clock, stop enemy threats, provide invulnerability or guarantee reconnection**. Explicit abandonment follows D1 and D1-C.2 once-only loss/protection, and D1-D.3 still prevents a parallel unresolved raid.

**D1-D.7 later requires a short consequence warning and second active confirmation to abandon; Cancel/Resume can never execute it. Still open:** Exact mobile prompt/touch/confirmation design, time remaining/expiry labels, recovery duration, stale-prompt/timeout races, state authority and background-world behavior. No code or new UI implementation is approved.

## Approved first-terminal-event interrupted-raid settlement (D1-D.5)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** An interrupted ordinary raid receives **one definitive authoritative outcome and exactly one immediate settlement when its first legitimate terminal event is established**. If the **original raid deadline** expires first, establish and settle its authentic **timeout at that time**, **without waiting for the recovery window to expire**. If the **bounded recovery window** expires first while the raid remains unresolved, close safe resumption, establish the **legitimate final outcome from authoritative raid state** and settle once under D1/D1-C.2. The existence of the other clock never postpones settlement.

**Respect already-established results:** Confirmed extraction, death, explicit Abandon or an earlier legitimate timeout are not overwritten when a later timer/reconnect occurs. No late extraction, full-bag rescue, duplicate protected item, extra reward or extract-only mission credit. D1-D.3's new-raid lock ends once the authoritative final result exists; D1-D.4 shows that real result rather than a stale Resume/Abandon decision.

**D1-D.6 now resolves a genuine exact-time tie:** completed extraction wins against death or the original raid timeout at the same authoritative instant; incomplete extraction never counts and earlier death/timeout still fails. **Still open:** other same-tick ties including recovery-window expiry and resumption, authoritative time/event sequencing, suspended-world simulation, clock/persistence technology, recovery-window duration and UI. The design principle is **first legitimate authoritative terminal event**, not the first client message received. No gameplay code.

## Approved extraction wins an exact authoritative death/timeout tie (D1-D.6)

**Owner-approved, option B, 8 November 2026:** If a **fully completed extraction** occurs at **exactly the same authoritative instant** as death or the original raid timeout, count **successful extraction** and bank the **actual carried haul once**. **If death or timeout occurs earlier**, the raid fails with D1 loss and **D1-C.2's one protected eligible unit returned once**, not the exposed full bag. If extraction was already definitively completed earlier, later failure events never overwrite it.

**Not a grace period:** A started but **unfinished extraction countdown is not a success**. The equality rule grants **no extra raid time**, automatic extraction, invulnerability or protection. The original deadline still runs under D1-D.2; D1-D.5 still settles the first legitimate definitive outcome once. At a genuine equal-time extraction/death/timeout tie, the completed extraction **resolves that tie in the player's favor**, and there is no separate failure-side Secure Slot credit or duplicated haul.

**Still open:** Exact canonical timestamp/tick implementation, simultaneous **recovery-window expiry and reconnect** cases, world simulation during interruption, replay/latency protection, and detailed UX. No game code or tech solution is approved by this rule.

## Approved explicit consequence confirmation before Abandon (D1-D.7)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** When the player selects **Explicitly abandon** on D1-D.4's pending-raid return screen, first show a **concise, understandable loss confirmation**: the raid will **fail**, **exposed loot and risked deployed equipment will be lost**, and **at most the one selected eligible Secure Slot unit survives** under D1-C.2 (none if no eligible item is selected). A **separate, affirmative Confirm abandonment** action is required to make this final. **Cancel, Resume, dismissing the warning or opening a menu must never abandon the raid**, and no destructive choice is selected automatically.

**Preserve D1-D:** Explicit confirmed abandonment settles as one genuine failure under D1 and D1-C.2; there is **no extra claim, full-bag recovery, refund or automatic extraction**. Reviewing the warning **does not pause the original raid timer, freeze damage, provide immunity or extend the recovery window**. If a legitimate authoritative outcome already exists when confirmation is attempted, display/keep that result and do not overwrite it or settle twice under D1-D.5/D1-D.6.

**Still open:** Exact touch UI/wording, button ordering, confirmation presentation, race handling against authentic timeout/death/extraction and technical persistence. This is not authorization for a game/UI code change.

## Approved verifiable combat danger during interruption (D1-D.8)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026, option A:** An **unexpectedly interrupted ordinary raid continues to expose the operator to legitimate combat risk**. Enemies, active attacks and environmental hazards **may still harm or kill the player even without input**, but only where combat outcomes are supported by **authoritative, verifiable raid state**. Deliberately force-closing or reconnecting **must not restore an artificially safe/frozen version** of an already dangerous fight. This defines what outcomes are legitimate, **not** which server or client architecture must perform simulation.

**No fabricated outcomes, no immunity:** A hypothetical, unverifiable enemy hit is **not permission to declare death**. When combat can be safely restored and verified, the player continues the same raid with authentic risk (D1-D). If it cannot be safely restored, a **legitimate final outcome** must be established from authoritative facts and **settled once** under D1/D1-C.2; a crash is not itself a death. Original raid time continues (D1-D.2); bounded recovery (D1-D.1), one pending raid (D1-D.3), first-terminal settlement (D1-D.5), exact completed-extraction tie (D1-D.6), and explicit Abandon confirmation (D1-D.7) remain unchanged. No free invulnerability, full-bag rescue, arbitrary rollback or guaranteed rejoin.

**D1-D.9 later rules out any newly automated operator dodge, movement, shooting or cover-seeking when input is unavailable; ordinary existing effects and verified external threats remain. Still open:** Handling last held input, enemy/projectile/hazard/actor simulation and physics, **authoritative validation and recoverability/fairness thresholds**, performance/battery costs, state/network technology, exact timing and mobile feedback. No continuous server world, dedicated simulation service, or game/UI code is authorized.

## Approved no autonomous operator defense during interrupted input (D1-D.9)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026, option A:** While an involuntary interruption prevents player input, the operator **receives no newly automated movement, dodging, shooting or pathfinding to cover**. Use the **last authoritatively validated position/stance and existing active state** as the starting point for truthful combat reconciliation; this does **not** freeze the world or undo subsequent legitimate effects. Existing **non-input-dependent effects continue under their normal game rules** when validated. No free defensive AI, automatic BIT support, pause immunity or guaranteed survival.

**D1-D.8 still governs external danger:** Enemies, attacks and hazards may still inflict **verified** damage/death. D1-D.2's original deadline runs in real time; D1-D safe same-raid resumption remains conditional on recoverability; terminal outcomes are settled once per D1-D.5/D1-D.6 and D1/C.2. Force-closing must not gain a favorable reset or fabricate a new command/hit. An interruption itself is neither a player-selected Abandon nor proof of death.

**Still open:** **What happens to held movement/aim/fire/interaction input at the instant of interruption**, buffering or release behavior, precise player physics and non-input-dependent effects, validation/fairness, world/combat simulation, network/persistence architecture, resume eligibility, recovery duration and UX. No actor AI or gameplay implementation approved.

## Commerce structure retained

**Approved M5 families:** disclosed deterministic resource packages, milestone Growth Fund, and permanent operator/BIT/bunker cosmetic ownership. Purchases may accelerate permitted meta progression after eligibility; they cannot buy extraction, replace required exploration or create a paid-only combat solution.

**Growth Fund structure retained:** one-time explicit purchase; real earned bunker milestones; free and paid tracks; late purchase with retroactive claims of eligible earned milestones. No subscription, login chore or purchased milestone completion. Its detailed contents, price and numbers remain proposals. A paid resource-bearing Fund is offered only after gameplay-built Fabricator L1; earlier achievements can still be recorded and claimed retroactively later. A free earned milestone reward is not a material purchase offer.

**UX hypotheses after eligibility:** missing-part panels pair a truthful free source/route with an optional disclosed purchase; paid goods go to home storage; building remains explicit. Exact placements, pack catalogs, caps, prices, rewards and production entitlement verification need separate design/validation. No commerce during raids, extraction or loss screens.

## Approved deep-raid experience (M21)

**Owner-approved direction, 7 November 2026:** Deeper areas of ordinary raids combine **richer tactical encounters**, meaningful route/positioning tradeoffs and **better probabilistic opportunities** for valuable loot. Reward outcomes remain uncertain and are only secured through actual extraction. Forward nodes enable pushing deeper but never block an early return; D1/D2 risk and voluntary extraction remain intact. Varied enemy combinations, readable counterplay and optional higher-risk routes are design approaches to test, not approved encounter blueprints or drop-rate numbers. Retain simple mobile controls and avoid solving difficulty solely through HP inflation. BIT's one foregrounded home hint and optional living-world in-raid detours (M20–M20C) must fit this same normal raid loop.

## Approved tactical combat foundation (M22)

**Owner-approved direction, 7 November 2026:** The core combat's tactical depth comes first from **enemy behavior and combinations, positioning, environmental geometry and readable counterplay**. An ordinary raid must be engaging with the **free Basic Carbine**, not only after a powerful upgrade or purchase. Weapons, gadgets, builds and meta progression add *alternative solutions* and tradeoffs; they do not substitute for good encounters or create a paid win condition. Combine enemy roles to raise the decision complexity in deeper areas (M21), while preserving mobile readability, voluntary extraction and loot risk. Exact enemy role sequencing, gadget UX, encounter layouts and power curves remain to be designed/tested, not pre-approved implementation details.

## Approved enemy learning curve (M23)

**Owner-approved direction, 7 November 2026:** Establish recognizable enemy archetypes quickly in real combat, with no long tutorial/card sequence. The early combat experience gives the player enough visual information and space to learn each threat's counterplay while using the Basic Carbine. Progressively recombine known roles, terrain, cover and player positioning to make deeper raids more tactically demanding without relying on HP inflation, extra required controls or paid equipment. BIT's coaching remains contextual and unobtrusive. Encounter order, statistics, readability thresholds and mix timings are still hypotheses to validate on phones.

## Approved readable, player-chosen difficulty (M24)

**Owner-approved, 7 November 2026:** The ordinary raid's risk curve comes primarily from **chosen route, depth, encounter and extraction timing**, and the danger should be understandable through enemy telegraphs, environments and BIT's light contextual cues. Do not covertly re-scale difficulty in response to player performance to force a target win rate. Preserve tactical learning and Basic Carbine viability (M22/M23), optional deeper reward opportunities (M21), true loot risk (D1) and voluntary early extraction (D2). **D3.8 is a narrow onboarding exception:** bounded, gradually more forgiving assistance after first-normal-raid failures, still genuinely failable; never automatic survival or loot protection. Existing adaptive-difficulty code is historical baseline, not the agreed target policy. Numerical tuning and exact danger-signaling UX remain open.

## Approved world-readable risk signals (M25)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** Players infer dangerous routes and encounters mainly from **the environment, visible enemy warnings and brief contextual BIT hints**. Do not replace this with numeric risk scores, explicit universal low/medium/high ratings or complete advance information. Preserve readable mobile tells and silent-play comprehension: players should have an informed choice to investigate, avoid, push deeper or extract, but should still be surprised by the living world. An unknown threat may surprise without causing unavoidable, untelegraphed damage. This extends M24's player-chosen difficulty, M22/M23's enemy-learning foundation and BIT's non-blocking guidance. The D3.8 early-failure support remains a separate first-normal-raid exception. Exact signals, timings and balancing need mobile playtests, not a code change in this design PR.

## Approved room-stage payoff (M26)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** Every major room's 2–3 progressive repairs **visibly change the home** but do **not** grant intermediate gameplay utility. The final completed stage activates the room's full established functions and gameplay rewards. Sum of stage costs equals the **unchanged original total**, not a surcharge or separate upkeep economy. **M37 requires the complete current-stage ingredient cost to be available in home storage and deliberately paid in one action, never incrementally deposited.** A completed stage consumes legitimately banked resources once and preserves its visible restoration, even if later raids fail. **Fabricator L1's functional reward and material-commerce eligibility occur only on full, earned completion**, and **Vault L1's one Secure Slot** is likewise not available early. Core L1's approved FTUE activation is unaffected. Older intermediate-utility proposals are superseded by M26; precise stage visuals and ingredient partitioning remain unapproved.

## Approved post-Fabricator project choice (M27)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** After **fully earning and building Fabricator L1 through play**, the player **chooses accessible Vault L1 or accessible BIT Bay L1** as the next primary Bunker Project. BIT may suggest a goal, never select or require it; show only **one actively tracked Project** at a time. If world/access prerequisites are not met, no additional unlock is implied by this choice. Vault L1 offers permanent storage improvements and, **only on full room completion**, the one approved Secure Slot. BIT Bay L1 upgrades the companion's support hardware and pays off **only after final completion**; BIT was already met and accompanies the player before Bay L1. M28 locks its first **capability family** to limited active combat support (for example a brief enemy distraction or short defensive assist); M29 selects brief distraction of one dangerous enemy as the first L1 effect; M30 requires a simple player-triggered contextual action, and M31 lets BIT select the most immediately threatening enemy after that action; M32 makes successful distractions reusable after a meaningful cooldown; M34 requires every enemy type to respond, although M33 heavy/specialist resistance may produce weaker, shorter or different effects. Exact input, enemy reaction mapping, threat-priority logic, timer/reset, UI and balance remain open. Both rooms retain 2–3 visual-only stages within the existing total material cost under M26, and D1/D2 extraction risk is unchanged. Past fixed `Fabricator → Vault → BIT Bay` modeling sequences are **historical scenarios, not required progression**. No new currencies, purchases or forced quests. **M36 further approves freely switching the one tracked Project among accessible rooms without losing completed restoration steps or invested materials.**

## Approved BIT Bay L1 combat support (M28)

**Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** On **full completion of BIT Bay L1**, BIT gains **limited active combat support**, whose first concrete L1 effect is now selected by M29: **briefly distracting one dangerous enemy**. Short-lived defense was an earlier alternative example and is **not** an additional L1 ability. Scanning/intelligence as the primary first BIT Bay completion reward is **superseded**; BIT still accompanies, speaks, gives contextual hints and signals optional living-world opportunities before any Bay upgrade. Support must preserve the primacy of **enemy behavior, terrain, player positioning and the free Basic Carbine** (M22–M25). **Owner-approved, 8 November 2026:** A bunker restoration step may be built only with **all of its ingredient quantities already available in the home stash**. - **A — Release unavailable input to neutral promptly (recommended).** On an authoritative interruption/loss-of-input transition, **stop repeating held move, aim/fire and interaction commands**, rather than continuing them indefinitely.
